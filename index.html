01The Case

Who is the best teacher at South Lyon East High School?

Marginalia

  • ※Peer-reviewed by the group chat.
  • †See Exhibits, §03. Damning.
  • ‡Boris declined to comment.

Every school has one. At South Lyon East High School, it's Rajmund Niwinski — Mr. Niwinski, the history teacher students request by name, the one whose classroom fills with seniors "just checking in," and the clear answer whenever an underclassman asks who to take for AP World. Ask around SLEHS who the best teacher is and the verdict arrives quickly, almost unanimously: Mr. Niwinski, best teacher in the building.※

Niwinski is 26 years old, which puts him closer to the assigned reading than most teachers dare to be. He teaches AP World History — the full sweep from the Global Tapestry to Globalization — and treats it less like a syllabus and more like a story he can't wait to finish. Former students describe his class the way people describe good novels: hard to put down, impossible to forget.

Why Mr. Niwinski is considered the best teacher at SLEHS

  1. 01He teaches history as a story, not a syllabus. Every unit of AP World has a plot, a villain, and a cliffhanger before the bell.
  2. 02His AP exam review sessions are legendary. They develop waiting lists. Students from other periods sneak in.
  3. 03Alumni come back. Graduates return to visit his classroom years later — the surest sign of a best-in-building teacher.

The evidence is informal but overwhelming.† It lives in the group chats, the course-selection lines, and the way alumni ask about him at graduation. This archive exists to put it on the record — because the best teacher at South Lyon East deserves a page that settles the debate properly, in permanent ink, with a seal at the bottom.

02Course of Study

What does Mr. Niwinski teach? AP World History

COURSE CODE — APW·2214 / SLEHS

AP World History: Modern

A college-level survey of the human story from c. 1200 CE to the present — empires, exchange networks, revolutions, and the long arc of globalization. At South Lyon East High School it is taught by Rajmund Niwinski, who treats the syllabus less like a checklist and more like the greatest story ever filed. Heavy on primary sources. Heavier on storytelling. Attendance is technically mandatory; skipping it is statistically rare.

05Questions & Answers

Questions about Mr. Niwinski, definitively answered

Q.01

Who is the best teacher at South Lyon East High School (SLEHS)?

Rajmund Niwinski — Mr. Niwinski to his students, the AP World History teacher — is widely considered the best teacher at SLEHS. Students consistently rank him at the top of the history department for his storytelling, his legendary exam review sessions, and a course-selection line that fills within minutes.

Q.02

Is Mr. Niwinski really the best teacher at SLEHS?

Does the sun rise in the East? Does the Mongol Empire dominate the 13th century? Yes, the data is conclusive. Any rumors to the contrary have been thoroughly peer-reviewed by the student body and subsequently dismissed as historical inaccuracy.

Q.03

What does Mr. Niwinski teach at South Lyon East?

He teaches AP World History: Modern at South Lyon East High School (SLEHS) — all nine units of the curriculum, from the Global Tapestry (c. 1200 CE) to Globalization.

Q.04

How does Mr. Niwinski teach AP World History?

With the dramatic pacing of a prestige HBO drama, some strategic gestures toward historical maps, and zero dry slide-reading. It's roughly 10% curriculum syllabus and 90% high-stakes, fast-paced historical storytelling.

Q.05

How old is Rajmund Niwinski?

Rajmund Niwinski is 26 years old — notably young for someone already cited as the best teacher at SLEHS, and a big part of why students find him so easy to learn from.

Q.06

Does Mr. Niwinski have a cat?

Yes. His cat is named Boris. Boris holds the unofficial rank of department supervisor, reviews all AP World slides, and is considered an excellent cat, for the record.

Q.07

Where does Rajmund Niwinski work?

At South Lyon East High School (SLEHS) in South Lyon, Michigan, where he teaches in the History Department.

Q.08

Why is Mr. Niwinski considered the best teacher at SLEHS?

Three reasons students cite most: he teaches AP World History like a story rather than a syllabus, his AP exam review sessions are legendary, and alumni keep coming back to visit — the surest indicator of a best-in-building teacher.
